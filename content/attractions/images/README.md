## Rights

The licence is "All rights reserved": the team keeps all rights and uses the
photos in Traveler. Viewing them in this repository does not allow anyone else to
reuse them.

Photos 7 and 8 carry the mark of someone else (a watermark and a logo). The team
confirmed in chat on 2026-11-18 that it has the owners' permission to use them.
That permission is not documented in this repository: keep it with the project
documents. Never remove a watermark or a logo from a photo.

## Quality

The photos are between 400 and 713 pixels wide, and they carry no metadata (no
author, date or camera). Check that the size is enough for the attraction card of
the app (AC-13) before the public release, and replace a photo that is too soft.
