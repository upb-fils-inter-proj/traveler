## Description (RO)

Cazinoul Sinaia se află în Parcul Dimitrie Ghica. A fost ridicat la începutul anilor 1910, din inițiativa regelui Carol I, după planurile arhitectului Petre Antonescu. A funcționat ca cazino, cu mese de ruletă, iar George Enescu a cântat la festivitățile de inaugurare din 1913. Astăzi clădirea găzduiește evenimente și conferințe, iar sala de teatru are 400 de locuri. Cazinoul este monument istoric.

## Description (EN)

The Sinaia Casino stands in Dimitrie Ghica Park. It was built in the early 1910s at the initiative of King Carol I, to plans by the architect Petre Antonescu. It operated as a gambling casino with roulette tables, and George Enescu played at its inauguration events in 1913. Today the building hosts events and conferences, and its theatre hall has 400 seats. The Casino is a listed historic monument.

## Unverified (not used)

- Opening date and year: English Wikipedia and Orange say 1913, Romanian Wikipedia says 5 July 1912, and the official site says 8/21 July 1912 for the opening and 1913 for the building's inauguration. The sources disagree, so only "early 1910s" and Enescu's 1913 concert are used.
- Start of construction: English Wikipedia says 1912, Romanian Wikipedia says 1911 in the text (and 1913 in its infobox).
- Architectural style: English Wikipedia says Neo-Romanian, Romanian Wikipedia says neoclassical.
- Address: the official site and Romanian Wikipedia say Bd. Carol I nr. 2, while OpenStreetMap names Bulevardul Ferdinand.
- Role of Baron de Marcay (linked to the Monte Carlo casino): Orange calls him the financier, English Wikipedia calls him the main contractor and a shareholder in the Monte Carlo casino. The roles differ, so he is not named in the description.
- Number of roulette tables (13, later 20) and the later owners: Orange only.
- End of gambling in 1947: Romanian Wikipedia (and English Wikipedia, "late 1940s"), but both are Wikipedia, so not independent.
- Visiting programme and tickets: change often and are only on the official site. Not stored.
