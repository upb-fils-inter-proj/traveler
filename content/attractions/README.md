# Attractions of Sinaia

> **Status: proposed, pending team agreement.** The list below is a proposal.
> It is not final until the team has agreed on it (issue #24, task #25). Change
> the names or the order here in the same pull request that records the
> decision, and rename the files to match.

The list has 10 places in or immediately around the town of Sinaia (Prahova
County) that are open to visitors, well known, and different from each other:
two royal castles, a monastery, a casino, a composer's villa, a park, a local
museum, a railway station and two mountain stations.

## Proposed list

| No. | Name (RO) | Name (EN) | Why it belongs | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Castelul Peleș | Peleș Castle | The royal summer residence of King Carol I, started in 1873, and the best-known sight of the town | researched, [file](01-peles-castle.md) |
| 2 | Castelul Pelișor | Pelișor Castle | An Art Nouveau castle built from 1899 for the heir to the throne, close to Peleș | researched, [file](02-pelisor-castle.md) |
| 3 | Mănăstirea Sinaia | Sinaia Monastery | Founded in the 1690s by Mihail Cantacuzino; the town is named after it | researched, [file](03-sinaia-monastery.md) |
| 4 | Cazinoul Sinaia | Sinaia Casino | A casino of the early 1910s in the central park, now an events and conference venue | researched, [file](04-sinaia-casino.md) |
| 5 | Casa Memorială „George Enescu” (Vila Luminiș) | George Enescu Memorial House (Luminiș Villa) | The villa the composer built in 1923-1926, a memorial house since 1995 | researched, [file](05-george-enescu-memorial-house.md) |
| 6 | Parcul Dimitrie Ghica | Dimitrie Ghica Park | The central park, surrounded by historic buildings, with the Casino and a public monument from 1935 | proposed |
| 7 | Muzeul Orașului Sinaia (Castelul Știrbey) | Sinaia City Museum (Știrbey Castle) | The oldest civil building of the town (1874-1875), a museum with 13 exhibition spaces since 2015 | proposed |
| 8 | Gara Sinaia | Sinaia Railway Station | A listed station ensemble: the royal station of 1870 and the passenger station of 1930-1940 | proposed |
| 9 | Cota 1400 | Cota 1400 | The middle station of the cable car from the resort to the Bucegi Mountains, with restaurants, terraces and easy walks | proposed |
| 10 | Cota 2000 | Cota 2000 | The top station on the Bucegi plateau, with ski runs in winter and hiking routes in summer | proposed |

The first five are the batch researched under task #26. The order follows the
research order, not any ranking.

## How the list was chosen

The candidates come from pages read on 2026-11-18. The reasons in the table are
taken from them.

- Wikivoyage, "See" section of Sinaia: <https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Sinaia>
- English Wikipedia, landmarks and transport of Sinaia: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinaia>
- Romanian Wikipedia, list of listed monuments of Sinaia: <https://ro.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinaia>
- Cotidianul, on Știrbey Castle and the city museum: <https://www.cotidianul.ro/castelul-stirbey-aristocratia-aerului/>
- InfoContact, stations and sections of the Sinaia cable cars (2026): <https://www.infocontact.ro/telecabina-sinaia-program-pret-2026/>

Items 1 to 5 have been checked against two sources each (see their files).
Items 6 to 10 were found in the pages above and still need the same check.

## Points for the team to decide

- **Item 7, Sinaia City Museum.** The pages read confirm that the museum exists
  and is in Știrbey Castle, but no official page with opening times was found.
  Confirm that it is open to visitors before the list is agreed.
- **Items 9 and 10, Cota 1400 and Cota 2000.** Two lift systems with different
  operators run from the town to Cota 1400 and on to Cota 2000 (same InfoContact
  page). The place of the QR codes and the operating season have to be decided.
  Cota 2000 is on the mountain, not in the town.
- **Closing days.** peles.ro announces that Peleș and Pelișor are closed from
  2 November to 1 December 2026 (<https://peles.ro>). A check-in at those two
  places will not be possible in that period.

## Reserve candidates

Places found in the same pages that could replace an item above. They have not
been checked further.

- Eagles of Freedom Plaza, opened in 2015 in memory of American soldiers who
  died in Romania in the Second World War (English Wikipedia, Sinaia).
- Heroes' Cemetery and the Franz Joseph and Saint Anne Cliffs (English Wikipedia,
  Sinaia). Both are listed without detail there.
- Foișor Castle, part of the Peleș castle ensemble in the list of monuments
  (Romanian Wikipedia, Sinaia). It was not checked whether visitors can enter.

Left out on purpose: Ialomița Cave, about 9.5 km in a straight line from the
town centre, in the Bucegi Mountains (Wikivoyage gives its coordinates).
